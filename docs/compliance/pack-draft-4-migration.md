## What happens to stored reviewer decisions

A reviewer decision is bound to the pack version and a digest of the criterion it was made about.
When the pack version moves, a decision recorded under the previous pack is **carried forward**
only if the criterion's definition is unchanged: the digest recomputed with the old pack version
reproduces the stored one. Otherwise it is reported **stale** (`pack_version_changed`) and not
applied, and a reviewer records it again against the current wording. A changed title never
makes a decision stale.

- Unchanged, decisions carry forward: 16
- Changed, decisions go stale: 14
- Replaced by a new criterion, decisions go stale: 1
- Retired, decisions go stale: 0
- New in vecshield-vector-store-draft-4: 2

Source records are not part of the digest. A corrected note, date or legal status on a cited
source does not make a decision stale; adding or removing a cited source does.
